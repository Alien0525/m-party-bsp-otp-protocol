M-Party BSP Protocol

Headless Simulation Test Suite

Wastage = (N − padsUsed) / N. Baseline = (m−1)/m — without BSP jumps, only 1 of m arenas is ever reachable, so m=3 wastes 66.7%, m=4 wastes 75%, m=8 wastes 87.5%, etc. The goal of the protocol is to beat that number. d ∈ {3,4,5} (fixed) · N ∈ {100,128,256,512,1000,1024,2048} · m ∈ {3,4} for small N; {3,4,5,8,10,16} for large N. Monte Carlo averaged over 50 trials.

What each test measures →
MC Monte Carlo — random party selection; models realistic async message arrival. Best estimate of average-case wastage.
RR Round Robin — perfectly uniform turn-taking; measures protocol efficiency under ideal fairness-guaranteed scheduling.
FL Single Flood — one party sends all messages; stress-tests worst-case imbalanced load and early deadlock conditions.
COL Collision Force — strict A↔B alternation; maximises jump frequency to stress-test the BSP arena-relocation mechanism.
